About the book

Formation begins deeper than behaviour.

The Reverse Gospel explores the movement of Galatians 5 through the inner life, tracing how thought, desire, character, and relationship participate in the slow formation of love. The invitation is less about mastering a sequence and more about becoming attentive to the ways God is forming a life that bears fruit.

Who it is for

A resource for reflection, discipleship, and conversation.

This book may serve individuals, small groups, ministry leaders, parents, and anyone seeking language for the relationship between spiritual formation, character, and lived faith.

It can be read on its own or used alongside guided reflection and conversation as part of a wider formation pathway.
