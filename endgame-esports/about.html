About EñDgame

Growth beyond the game

We work with families, players, and teams who want the time they spend around gaming to build skills and strengthen relationships.

Why we started

Bret Chapman founded EñDgame eSports through years of mentoring players and developing leaders in online communities. He saw how much young people could learn through competition when a coach paid attention to how they handled pressure, communicated, and made room for others.

That same work raised questions for parents. What happens in the game that we rarely get to see? How can we listen when a child meets conflict online? Those questions shape how we work with families and the organizations that support them.

What EñD means

Our name holds two goals that guide the way we coach.

See how far you can go

Practise with purpose, learn from feedback, and build the skills your team can rely on under pressure.

Carry it into life beyond play

Notice how you respond to setbacks, take responsibility, and support others. Bring those habits into the relationships and communities you’re part of.
